# STEP and TMUA content framework

Framework fixed before content authoring on 2026-11-13. This is an editorial learning sequence mapped to official specifications, not an official endorsement or a prediction of question frequency. Coverage at the topic-family level does not imply exhaustive treatment of every specification bullet.

## TMUA: 15 topics

Both papers use Section 1: MM1-MM8 and M1-M7. Paper 2 additionally uses Section 2: Arg1-Arg4, Prf1-Prf5 and Err1-Err2. Paper 2 is not a separate advanced-mathematics syllabus. Each paper has 20 multiple-choice questions in 75 minutes; calculators and a formula booklet are unavailable. Wrong answers do not incur a penalty.

| Subject / topic | Official mapping | Prerequisites / learning target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations & data / Number, units & bounds | M1, M2 | Exact arithmetic, surds, prime factors, unit conversions, rounding intervals |
| Foundations & data / Ratio, proportion & models | M3 | Number; multiplicative changes, scale and inverse proportion |
| Foundations & data / Statistics & representations | M6 | Number and ratio; summaries, unequal-width histograms, cumulative frequency and correlation |
| Foundations & data / Probability & counting | M2.5, M7 | Ratio; sample spaces, trees, dependence and conditional probability |
| Algebra & functions / Expressions & equations | MM1.1-MM1.6, M4 | Number; quadratics, polynomial division, inequalities and valid transformations |
| Algebra & functions / Functions & graphs | MM1.7, MM8 | Expressions; domains, composition, transformations, modulus and intersections |
| Algebra & functions / Sequences & binomial expansions | MM2 | Expressions; arithmetic/geometric sums, recurrence and positive-integer binomial expansion |
| Algebra & functions / Exponentials & logarithms | MM5 | Functions; laws, substitution and domain restrictions |
| Geometry & calculus / Coordinate geometry | MM3, M4.9-M4.10, M5.10 | Expressions; lines, circles, tangency and intersections |
| Geometry & calculus / Geometry & measurement | M5 | Ratio and coordinates; similarity, circle angles, 3-D measurement, vectors and bearings |
| Geometry & calculus / Trigonometry | MM4, M5.18 | Geometry and functions; exact values, intervals, radians, sine/cosine rules |
| Geometry & calculus / Differentiation & integration | MM6-MM7, MM8.5, M4.14 | Functions; rational powers, tangents, stationary points, signed area, FTC and trapezia |
| Logic & proof / Statements & quantifiers | Arg1-Arg4 | Expressions; implication, converse, contrapositive, necessity, sufficiency and negation |
| Logic & proof / Proof & counterexamples | Prf1-Prf5 | Statements; deduction, cases, contradiction, ordering arguments and conjectures |
| Logic & proof / Errors & equivalent reasoning | Err1-Err2 | Proof and algebra; invalid cancellation, squaring, inverse functions and hidden assumptions |

TMUA boundaries: no calculus from first principles; no requirement to differentiate trigonometric, exponential or logarithmic functions, use product/quotient/chain rules, or integrate by parts. Binomial expansions have positive integer exponents. Change of logarithm base is not required. Formal truth tables and symbolic logic notation are not required. Probability and GCSE statistics are explicitly included. Illustrations may use symbolic shorthand, always explained in words.

## STEP: 22 topics

STEP 2 assumes the Mathematics 1 content; STEP 3 assumes both Mathematics 1 and STEP 2. Mathematics 1 is assumed knowledge, not a currently offered STEP 1 paper. Each paper has 8 pure, 2 mechanics and 2 probability/statistics questions in 3 hours. The best six answers contribute to the score; each question is worth 20 marks. No calculator or formula booklet is issued. Topic groups below are editorial; the three sections are official.

| Subject / topic | Scope and official pages | Prerequisites / learning target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pure / Algebra & proof | Mathematics 1 pp7-8; STEP 2 p21 | Deduction, induction, inequalities, root transformations and partial fractions |
| Pure / Functions, curves & numerical methods | Mathematics 1 pp8-9,12,15; STEP 2 p21 | Algebra; inverse graphs, conics, exponential/logarithmic models, root location and iteration |
| Pure / Sequences, sums & binomial expansions | Mathematics 1 p10; STEP 2 p21 | Algebra and proof; telescoping, recurrence bounds, rational binomial expansions and the exponential series |
| Pure / Trigonometry | Mathematics 1 p11 | Algebra and graphs; compound angles, general solutions, small angles and auxiliary angles |
| Pure / Differentiation & curve analysis | Mathematics 1 p13; STEP 2 p22 | Functions and trigonometry; product/chain rules, implicit/parametric derivatives and inverse trigonometry |
| Pure / Integration & reduction | Mathematics 1 p14; STEP 2 p22 | Differentiation; substitution, parts, partial fractions, improper limits and reduction formulae |
| Pure / Complex numbers | STEP 2 p20; STEP 3 p25 | Quadratics and trigonometry; conjugates and loci (2/3); de Moivre and roots of unity (3) |
| Pure / Matrices & transformations | STEP 2 p21; STEP 3 p25 | Simultaneous equations; 2-by-2 inverses, composition and invariant lines (2/3); 3-by-3 systems (3) |
| Pure / Vectors & geometry | Mathematics 1 p15; STEP 2 p22; STEP 3 p26 | Coordinates; lines and dot products (2/3); planes, cross products and distances (3) |
| Pure / Maclaurin series & applications | STEP 3 pp25-26 | Differentiation and binomial series; convergence, leading terms, curve lengths and volumes |
| Pure / Polar coordinates & hyperbolic functions | STEP 3 pp26-27 | Trigonometry, exponentials and integration; polar area and inverse hyperbolic substitutions |
| Pure / Differential equations | Mathematics 1 pp13-14; STEP 3 p28 | Separable equations (2/3); integrating factors, auxiliary equations, forcing and coupled equations (3) |
| Mechanics / Forces & motion | Mathematics 1 pp16-17 | Vectors and calculus; kinematics, projectiles, Newton's laws, connected particles and friction |
| Mechanics / Statics & centres of mass | Mathematics 1 p17; STEP 3 p29 | Force balance; moments and toppling (2/3); composite and integrated centres of mass (3) |
| Mechanics / Energy, momentum & collisions | STEP 2 p23; STEP 3 p29 | Motion; work, power, energy and direct restitution (2/3); impulse and oblique impacts (3) |
| Mechanics / Elastic strings & springs | STEP 2 p23 | Energy and forces; Hooke's law, energy, slack/taut phases and equilibrium |
| Mechanics / Circular motion & oscillations | STEP 3 pp28-29 | Motion, energy and differential equations; radial/tangential equations, contact, SHM and variable forces |
| Probability & statistics / Conditional probability & expectation | Mathematics 1 pp18-19 | Counting and algebra; sample spaces, conditioning, independence and discrete moments |
| Probability & statistics / Discrete distributions & models | Mathematics 1 p19; STEP 2 p24 | Conditional probability; binomial, discrete uniform and Poisson with model assumptions |
| Probability & statistics / Continuous distributions | Mathematics 1 p19; STEP 2 p24 | Integration; densities, CDFs, uniform and Normal models, moments and approximations |
| Probability & statistics / Sampling & hypothesis testing | Mathematics 1 pp18-19 | Distributions; sampling, summaries, binomial tests and Normal mean tests with known variance |
| Probability & statistics / Independent variables & transformations | STEP 3 p30 | Expectation and continuous distributions; variance algebra, CDF transformation and functions of random variables |

Scope safeguards: de Moivre, complex roots, general Maclaurin expansions, plane/cross-product geometry, polar/hyperbolic methods and nonseparable differential equations are marked STEP 3. The exponential series and inverse-trigonometric differentiation already belong to STEP 2. General rigid-body dynamics, moments of inertia and generating functions are not added as requirements. The binomial-to-Normal/Poisson approximations are STEP 2 knowledge. A broad topic with mixed scope marks the individual STEP 3 techniques explicitly.

## Authoring and integration contract

1. Keep all seven existing TMUA and eight existing STEP topic routes. Add topics around them; preserve BMO/BMT content and University Interview scaffolds.
2. Each topic receives at least four bilingual techniques with a Chinese explanation, core conclusion, method, worked example, pitfall and searchable keywords. Mathematics uses the existing explicit TeX renderer.
3. Each topic receives two independently authored exercises with stable IDs, Chinese hints and English worked solutions. TMUA exercises include answer choices and a single answer; STEP exercises use linked parts. These are labelled original drills, not official past-paper questions, full mocks or calibrated exam difficulty.
4. Link official specifications, support notes and paper archives from the collections. Do not imply that an archive link makes an original exercise official.
5. Display scope, prerequisites and the topic map before the teaching content. The website data model must keep framework metadata separate from authored notes.
6. Run the existing content, formula, storage and keyword checks with scoped assertions for the newly authorised collections; add checks for framework coverage, prerequisites, original question IDs and MCQ answers. Inspect representative pages and run the existing multi-width browser checks when available.
